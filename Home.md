# StoryBoard

A shareable Obsidian vault of author templates — writing first, then editing, then marketing.

## Start here

1. Duplicate [[Projects/Project Dashboard Template|Project Dashboard Template]] for your manuscript.
2. Optional warm-up: [[01 Writing Templates/Idea to Plot Template|Idea to Plot]] → [[01 Writing Templates/Project Brief Template|Project Brief]].
3. Build characters, antagonist, settings, and [[01 Writing Templates/Plot Beat Sheet Template|Save the Cat–style beats]].
4. Draft with [[01 Writing Templates/Scene Card Template|Scene Cards]] and track progress in [[01 Writing Templates/Writing Goals Template|Writing Goals]].
5. When drafting cools, use [[02 Editing Templates/Beta Reader Worksheet|Beta Reader Worksheet]] and other editing templates.
6. When you are ready to share publicly, move into Marketing templates.

## How to use with Templater

If you use the Templater community plugin, point its template folder at `01 Writing Templates` (or whichever lane you use most). Duplicate a template into `Projects/` or a manuscript subfolder when you start filling it in — leave the originals blank for reuse.

## Contribution note

This vault is meant to be borrowed, remixed, and shared with other writers. Keep personal manuscript notes in your own private vault or a private fork.
