# StoryBoard

A shareable Obsidian vault of author templates — writing first, then editing, then marketing.

## Start here

1. Duplicate [[Projects/Project Dashboard Template|Project Dashboard Template]] for your manuscript.
2. Fill a [[01 Writing Templates/Project Brief Template|Project Brief]] and build characters, settings, and scene cards from there.
3. When drafting cools, move into [[02 Editing Templates/Manuscript Feedback Template|Editing]] templates.
4. When you are ready to share the work publicly, use [[03 Marketing Templates/Content Idea Template|Marketing]] templates.

## How to use with Templater

If you use the Templater community plugin, point its template folder at `01 Writing Templates` (or create templates from any lane as needed). Duplicate a template into `Projects/` or a manuscript subfolder when you start filling it in — leave the originals blank for reuse.

## Contribution note

This vault is meant to be borrowed, remixed, and shared with other writers. Keep personal manuscript notes in your own private vault or a private fork.
