# StoryBoard

A shareable Obsidian vault of author templates — writing first, then drafting, then editing, then marketing.

## Start here

1. Duplicate [[Projects/Project Dashboard Template|Project Dashboard Template]] for your book.
2. Plan with [[01 Writing Templates/Idea to Plot Template|Idea to Plot]] → [[01 Writing Templates/Project Brief Template|Project Brief]] → beats/characters.
3. Draft prose in [[02 Manuscript/Manuscript Guide|02 Manuscript]] using the [[02 Manuscript/Chapter Template|Chapter Template]].
4. Track progress in [[01 Writing Templates/Writing Goals Template|Writing Goals]].
5. When drafting cools, use [[03 Editing Templates/Beta Reader Worksheet|Beta Reader Worksheet]] and other editing templates.
6. When you are ready to share publicly, move into [[04 Marketing Templates/Market Research Template|Marketing]].

## How to use with Templater

If you use the **Templater** community plugin, point its template folder at `01 Writing Templates` (or `02 Manuscript` when inserting chapter shells). Duplicate templates into a project folder before filling them in — leave the originals blank for reuse.

## Contribution note

This vault is meant to be borrowed, remixed, and shared with other writers. Keep personal manuscript notes in your own private vault or a private fork.
