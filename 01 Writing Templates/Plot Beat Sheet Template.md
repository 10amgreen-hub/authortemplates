## Act III — Synthesis (77–100%)

### Finale (77–97%) — Five-Point Finale

1. **Gather the team / make the plan**
2. **Execute the plan**
3. **High Tower Surprise** — plan fails; almost defeated
4. **Dig deep down** — faith / theme enacted; new strength
5. **Execute the new plan** — resolve action + complete the arc
